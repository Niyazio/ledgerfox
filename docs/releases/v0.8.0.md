## What's new in LedgerFox 0.8.0

**Everything about a product on one page**
- Click any product in Products & stock to see its stock in each branch and warehouse, batches with expiry and where they came from, production runs and their costs, every sale and purchase, and how each invoice and bill was paid (cash or bank) and what is still outstanding.
- See the profit and margin, the best customers, suppliers with their lowest and highest rates, the last 12 months, and sales by branch. **Sell** and **Buy** start a new invoice or bill with the product already filled in.

**Customer and supplier pages**
- Each customer or supplier page shows what they bought or supplied, what they paid by cash and by bank, what is outstanding and overdue, and the profit on their invoices. It has **Documents**, **Products** and **Payments** tabs.
- Every name, invoice, bill and payment on these pages opens its own page.

**Countries**
- States, emirates and regions follow the country: the UAE shows its emirates, Saudi Arabia its regions, and so on. Indian states no longer appear for other countries.
- Choosing a customer's or supplier's country sets its currency, which you can still change.

**Education mode**
- Practice companies are now given by LedgerFox to approved teachers, institutes and trainers. They are free on up to 10 devices.
- Student packs of 25, 50 or 100 run for 6 months.
- Each organisation can hold one demo or practice company.
