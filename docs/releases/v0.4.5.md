## What's new in LedgerFox 0.4.5

**Education mode: free practice companies for students and beginners**
- Tick **Practice company (education mode)** when creating a company.
- Free forever, with **every feature** (POS, payroll, GST reports, manufacturing…), whatever your plan.
- Like Tally's educational mode, entries can be dated only the **1st, 2nd and last day of a month**.
- Printouts and PDFs carry a "Practice — not a valid tax document" watermark, and Excel/CSV downloads say "practice company".
- E-invoice and e-way bill files, verification QR codes and WhatsApp/email sharing are switched off.
- A learning-only organisation can hold a class of up to 50 users.

**Desktop**
- Clearer steps to start your 30-day free trial from the desktop app ("Create free account").
