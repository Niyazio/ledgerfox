## What's new in LedgerFox 0.4.1

**Keyboard shortcuts (Tally keys)**
- Vouchers: F8 sales invoice, Ctrl+F8 credit note, Alt+F8 delivery challan, F9 purchase bill, Ctrl+F9 debit note, F6 receipt, F5 payment, F7 journal
- Create: Alt+B POS billing, Alt+Q quotation, Alt+C new customer, Alt+S new supplier, Alt+I new product
- On a form: Ctrl+S save, Ctrl+A (or Ctrl+Enter) save & post, Ctrl+P print the open bill or receipt, Esc closes a pop-up
- Alt+G or Ctrl+K: type to jump to any screen; Alt+H dashboard, Alt+Y daybook, Alt+R reports
- F1 (or the keyboard button in the top bar) shows the full list; the keys are also shown next to menu items

At the POS counter, F2 (search) and F9 (pay) keep working as before.
