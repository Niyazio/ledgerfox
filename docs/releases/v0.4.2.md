## What's new in LedgerFox 0.4.2

**Share bills as PDF files**
- **Share PDF** on every invoice, receipt and payslip (in Print preview, or Share → Send as PDF). On phones and in the Android app it opens the share sheet with the PDF attached: WhatsApp, Gmail, Drive and others. On a computer it saves the PDF so you can attach it.
- **PDF** button to save the file.
- A4, A5 and thermal-roll layouts. Long bills run over several pages without splitting an item. Hindi and Tamil labels are kept.
- In the Android app, which cannot print directly, share the PDF to a printing app.

**Subscriptions (website)**
- Clearer messages when Razorpay refuses a payment. If auto-renew is not available, a "Pay once instead" button appears.
- The set-up check on `/vendor` now also shows whether Razorpay Subscriptions (auto-renew) is switched on.
