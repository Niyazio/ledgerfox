## What's new in LedgerFox 0.4.9

**More types of business**
- **Jewellery / gold & silver shop:** sell gold and silver by weight in grams (to 3 decimals, e.g. 12.345 g), with separate rates for 24K, 22K and 18K, making charges, job work / repairs, accounts for old-gold exchange, and GST 3%. Put the hallmark (HUID) in the line description.
- Also new: clothing / textiles / boutique, mobile / electronics shop, medical store / pharmacy, hardware / paints / building materials, automobile garage / spare parts, salon / spa / gym, agri inputs / fertiliser / seeds, furniture / home décor, and real estate / rentals.
- Each comes with suitable accounts, units, sample items, HSN codes and GST rates. Everything can be edited.

**Phones**
- The top bar fits on small screens: the company name no longer runs into the icons. Theme and updates are in the menu.
- Bill and receipt previews shrink to fit the phone screen, like a PDF viewer. Printing and PDFs stay full A4.

**Desktop app**
- Email, WhatsApp, phone and UPI links now open in the computer's own apps.
- A bill saved as PDF opens in its folder, ready to drag into WhatsApp or attach to an email.
- Camera scanning is allowed for LedgerFox's own pages (and asks once on Mac).
