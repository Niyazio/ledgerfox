## What's new in LedgerFox 0.4.8

**Automatic local copy on your computer**
- The desktop app now saves every company automatically to a folder on your computer (default: Documents\LedgerFox Data), while LedgerFox keeps working online with the cloud.
- Updated every 15 minutes while you are online, plus one copy per day for the last 30 days. No manual export or import.
- Choose another folder (for example C:\LedgerFox or a USB/backup drive) in **File → Change local copy folder**. **File → Save local copy now** (Ctrl+B) saves at once.
