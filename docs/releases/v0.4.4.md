## What's new in LedgerFox 0.4.4

**Desktop plans, the same as the website**
- One LedgerFox plan now covers the website and your computers. Open Settings → **Plan & billing** on this computer and **connect your LedgerFox account**: your plan (or your 30-day free trial) applies here too.
- Without a connected account, the desktop app runs on the **Free plan**: 1 user, 1 company, no POS, payroll, recurring invoices or GST return files. Billing, accounts, reports and exports always keep working, and your data stays on your computer.
- The app works offline and checks your plan online at least every 15 days.

**Fixes**
- Sign-up is no longer held up by the auto-pay step when adding a logo.
- Demo companies stay inside your own organisation.
- The free trial is given once per owner.
