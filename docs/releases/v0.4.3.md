## What's new in LedgerFox 0.4.3

**More languages**
- Malayalam (മലയാളം) and Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) for printed bills (Settings → Company → Bill language) and for the menu and POS screen (language picker in the top bar), joining Hindi and Tamil.

**Look and feel**
- New appearance picker (palette icon in the top bar):
  - **Theme:** Light, Dark or follow the device.
  - **Colour:** 8 accents (Blue, Indigo, Violet, Rose, Sunset, Emerald, Ocean, Graphite).
  - **Background:** Plain, Soft glow, Gradient, Dots or Warm paper.
- Printed bills and PDFs always stay plain white.

**Website subscriptions**
- 30-day free trial, started by setting up auto-pay (Razorpay takes ₹5 to check the UPI or card and refunds it).
- The UPI AutoPay ₹15,000 limit is explained when a plan costs more.
- Udyam number on subscription invoices.
