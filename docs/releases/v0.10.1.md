## What's new in LedgerFox 0.10.1

**Get paid by UPI, straight into your bank**
- Your customers see **Pay by UPI** on unpaid invoices in their customer portal and on the invoice link. Their UPI app opens with the amount filled in, and the money reaches your bank account in seconds with no commission. LedgerFox never holds your money.
- When the money arrives, press **Paid by UPI** on the invoice and enter the UPI reference (UTR). The invoice is marked paid.
- The same UTR cannot be recorded twice, so a payment is never counted twice.

Add your UPI ID in Settings → Company to switch it on.
