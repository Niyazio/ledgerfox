## What's new in LedgerFox 0.4.7

**Desktop app: online only, with offline billing**
- The desktop app always works online with your LedgerFox account, so your team sees the same data.
- No internet? Keep billing at the POS counter and making invoices, payments and expenses. They are sent and numbered automatically when the internet is back.
- **File → Save a backup to this computer** keeps a copy of your company's data.
- Data files from older versions are never deleted. They open view-only from **File → Open an old data file**.

**Terms, privacy and cookies**
- New Terms of service. Creating an account now asks you to accept the terms and the privacy policy.
- A cookie notice: advertising on the public pages loads only if you choose "Accept all".

**Education mode**
- Stronger "PRACTICE" watermarks on screen (with the signed-in name), and across every printed page and PDF.

**Demo companies**
- A demo company now comes with a paid plan (one at a time). To try every feature for free, create a practice company (education mode).
