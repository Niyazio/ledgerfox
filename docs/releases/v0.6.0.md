## What's new in LedgerFox 0.6.0

**Ready for the Gulf and international businesses**
- **Tax set up by country.** A new company gets its country's VAT / GST / sales tax rates ready to use (all editable):
  - **Gulf:** UAE 5%, Saudi Arabia 15% (both with reverse charge for imports), Bahrain 10%, Oman 5%; Qatar and Kuwait have no VAT.
  - **Elsewhere:** UK, EU countries, Singapore, Malaysia, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, South Africa, Kenya, Nepal, Sri Lanka and Bangladesh.
- **The right number for each country:** TRN (UAE), VAT number (Saudi Arabia, UK, EU), ABN (Australia) and so on. UAE TRNs and Saudi VAT numbers are checked when saved.
- **Gulf invoices:** "Tax Invoice" when you are VAT-registered, with your and your customer's VAT numbers and VAT shown as VAT.
- **Saudi Arabia (ZATCA):** "Simplified Tax Invoice" for customers without a VAT number, labels in English and Arabic, and the ZATCA QR code (seller, VAT number, time, total and VAT) on every issued invoice and credit/debit note.
- **Arabic on bills:** Settings → Bill language → English + Arabic, for any company.

**Subscriptions outside India**
- Plans are priced in **US dollars** for customers abroad, **dirhams (AED)** in the UAE and **riyals (SAR)** in Saudi Arabia. They pay by debit or credit card, with auto-renew, through Razorpay.
- No Indian GST is added for customers abroad (export of services). Customers in India pay in rupees as before.

**Other**
- Wording that named other accounting products has been replaced with neutral wording.
