## What's new in LedgerFox 0.10.0

**Cheques and interest**
- A cheque register for cheques received and issued, including post-dated cheques. A cheque touches your accounts only when it clears; a bounced cheque reverses the payment.
- Print cheques: date, payee, amount in words and figures, and A/c Payee. Adjust the positions once for your bank.
- Interest on overdue invoices, per customer, with a one-click interest invoice.

**Purchasing**
- Requests for quotation: ask several suppliers at once. They quote on their supplier portal, you compare prices side by side, and the best quote becomes a purchase order.
- Goods receipts: what arrived, and what was rejected after checking it.
- 3-way match: the order, the goods received and the bill are compared line by line.
- Landed cost: freight and customs added to the cost of the goods.

**Leads & CRM**
- A sales pipeline with calls, meetings and follow-up dates.
- One click turns a lead into a customer and a quotation.
- See which sources bring the most business.

**Service desk & AMC**
- Support tickets with due dates and time spent, billed in one click. Customers can raise tickets from their portal.
- AMC and service contracts billed month by month, quarter by quarter or yearly, with renewals.

**Control**
- Approval rules: who must approve what, step by step, before it is issued.
- Currency revaluation at month end.
- API keys to connect your website or online shop.

**User manual**
- A downloadable user manual with screenshots of every screen. Open it from the book icon at the top of the app or from the website.
