## What's new in LedgerFox 0.7.0

**The app in Arabic**
- Choose **العربية** in the language menu: the menu and the POS counter are in Arabic, and the layout runs right to left. Bills can print in English + Arabic.

**More of the Middle East**
- **New countries:** Egypt (VAT 14%), Jordan (sales tax 16%), Lebanon (VAT 11%), Iraq, Türkiye (KDV 20%), Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Libya and Pakistan join the Gulf countries. Each comes with its tax rates, currency and tax number set up.
- **Dinars:** amounts in Jordanian, Iraqi, Tunisian, Libyan, Kuwaiti, Bahraini and Omani dinars use 3 decimals.
- **VAT return report:** standard-rated, zero-rated, exempt and reverse-charge sales and purchases, VAT due, recoverable VAT and the net amount. For the UAE it uses the VAT 201 box numbers.
- **Hijri date** (Umm al-Qura) on Saudi invoices.
- **End-of-service gratuity** (Payroll) for the UAE and Saudi Arabia, under each country's labour law. On resignation, Saudi Arabia applies its reduced shares.

**Windows without the warning**
- LedgerFox can now also be published on the **Microsoft Store**. Store installs are signed by Microsoft and update themselves, with no "unknown publisher" warning.
