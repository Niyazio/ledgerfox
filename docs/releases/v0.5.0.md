## What's new in LedgerFox 0.5.0

**A fresh, modern look**
- Rounded "bubble" cards with soft shadows that lift on hover, gradient buttons, and a highlighted side menu.
- A see-through top bar, smooth scrolling, and gentle animations for pages, dialogs, the phone menu and messages. Turn on "reduce motion" in your device settings for no animation.

**Product QR codes and labels**
- Every product has a code (P000001…), created automatically and editable. Products added with a new company now get codes too, and existing products without a code have been numbered.
- The product form shows its QR code and barcode, with **Print labels** and **Download QR**.
- Label QR codes are now links: scan one with **any phone camera** while signed in and billing opens with that product added. Older labels keep working in the LedgerFox scanner.

**Billing with the phone camera**
- In POS, the camera stays open: scan product after product and each one is added to the bill, with a beep and a buzz.

**Android app updates inside the app**
- Tap **Update** and the app downloads the new version itself. Android then asks you to tap **Install**. No browser or GitHub page needed. Your data and sign-in are kept.
- The first time, Android may ask you to allow LedgerFox to install updates.
- This works from the next update after you install 0.5.0. To get 0.5.0 itself, download it once more from the website.

**Phones**
- Long product names and cards no longer make any screen wider than the phone.
