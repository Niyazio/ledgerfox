## What's new in LedgerFox 0.4.0

**GST and compliance**
- GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B files for the GST offline tool, with HSN summary
- e-Invoice (IRP) and e-way bill files; IRN and e-invoice QR print on the bill
- TDS on receipts and payments; GST reverse charge on supplier bills
- PF (ECR), ESI and Form 24Q files from payroll

**Billing and stock**
- POS counter billing (cash, UPI, card, split; held bills; thermal print)
- Proforma invoices, delivery challans, recurring invoices, credit limits
- Price lists (wholesale/dealer rates, quantity breaks)
- Batch numbers and expiry dates, with expiry alerts
- Manufacturing: bills of materials and production

**Everyday use**
- Ask LedgerFox: type questions like "sales this month" or "who owes me money?"
- Excel (.xlsx) download next to every CSV download
- Import customers and products from Excel
- Share invoices and reminders on WhatsApp or email
- Hindi and Tamil labels on bills; menu and POS in Hindi or Tamil
- Two-factor sign-in; accountant "Clients" page for many companies
- QR codes on bills can be switched off
- Update button in the top bar shows new versions and installs them
- Keyboard shortcuts like Tally: F8 sales, F9 purchase, F6 receipt, F5 payment, F7 journal, Ctrl+F8 credit note, Ctrl+F9 debit note; Ctrl+S save, Ctrl+A save & post, Ctrl+P print; Alt+G (or Ctrl+K) to jump to any screen; F1 shows the full list

**Fixes**
- Phone camera scanning now works everywhere: on iPhones, and on phones using the office PC's network link (take a photo of the code)
- Android app: Excel/CSV/JSON downloads open the share sheet (save to Downloads, Drive, WhatsApp)

**Android users of 0.3.0:** uninstall the old app once before installing 0.4.0 (the old one was signed with a temporary key). Your data stays on the office computer or in the cloud; download the phone copy again after installing.
