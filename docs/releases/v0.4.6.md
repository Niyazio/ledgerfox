## What's new in LedgerFox 0.4.6

**Desktop app works online with your website account**
- On first start (or once after updating), choose **Online with my LedgerFox account**. The app shows the LedgerFox website: sign in with the same email and password, and everyone in your business sees the same data.
- No internet? Screens you opened recently still show, and new invoices, payments, expenses, contacts and journal entries wait on this computer. They are sent automatically when the internet is back.
- Prefer to keep everything on this computer? Choose **File → Use a data file on this computer**. Your existing data file is never changed or deleted.

**No Free plan: renew to continue**
- When the free trial or a paid plan (plus 7 days of grace) ends, LedgerFox asks you to **renew to continue**. You can still view, print and export everything, but cannot add entries until you renew.
- In the desktop app's data-file mode, sign in to your LedgerFox account in Settings → Plan & billing to use your plan or start the 30-day free trial.
- Practice companies (education mode) stay free.
