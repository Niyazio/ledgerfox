## What's new in LedgerFox 0.9.0

**Switch features on or off for your kind of business**
- Settings → Features: a school, an agency or a clinic can hide barcode scanning, POS, stock, manufacturing and anything else it does not use. Nothing is deleted; switch a feature back on and everything is there.
- New companies start with sensible features for their business type.

**Contra entries**
- Cash deposited to the bank, cash withdrawn, and transfers between bank accounts, as one numbered entry that can be cancelled with a reversal.

**Audit checks and tax check**
- Reports → Audit checks: big cash payments and receipts, negative cash, duplicate supplier bills, late entries, manual journals to control accounts, missing invoice numbers, and ledgers that do not agree with their details.
- Reports → Tax check, also linked from GST returns: it finds a wrong GSTIN, a state that does not match the GSTIN, and products without HSN/SAC or a tax rate. Each one has a fix button.

**Supplier portal**
- Send a supplier a private link. They can see your purchase orders and accept or decline them, see their bills in your books, see your payments to them and see what you still owe.

**Education mode**
- LedgerFox can now allow a teacher more than one practice company.
- When access ends, a clear page shows the next step: start your own business with a 30-day free trial, open your companies, visit the website or contact us.

**Faster**
- Screens and saves need fewer trips to the database, and the server now runs close to the database.
